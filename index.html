Before our Vibe Coding Session

Everything below is setup only, so we can spend our time together building rather than troubleshooting.

⏱ About 45 minutes Finish before class
0 of 5 done

  1. 1. 💳 Get a paid Claude plan Required

    Claude Code does not work on the free plan. The Pro plan is enough for our session: about $20 per month, or $17 per month billed annually. Max, Team, and Enterprise plans work too.

    If ESSEC gives you a university or team Claude account, check whether Claude Code is already enabled on it before you pay for anything yourself.

    Can't get a paid plan? Team up with a classmate who has one. This is only our second lesson, so it's a great excuse to get to know someone new. Message a classmate, agree to share their account for the session, and plan to sit together in class.
